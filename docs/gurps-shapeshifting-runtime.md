# Approved core form transformations

Characters third printing B83–85 distinguishes a replacement racial template from
an activation flag. Campaign-authored `alternate-form` and `morph` transformation
paths use the existing proposal, director approval, treatment, compilation and
resource-rebase lifecycle. The native approved build must purchase the matching
capability; authored template costs must agree with its parameters and the actual
build cost difference. Core paths retain the capability, ownership, identity and
history. Alternate Form purchases count their authored forms; extra forms cost 15
points each, with the 90% premium applied only to the most expensive template.
Morph and less expensive Alternate Forms may select a template below the purchased
maximum. They are voluntary, reversible and require ten seconds of treatment.
Modifiers require their own implementation and are rejected by this core path.

HP and FP deficits scale with the new form's maxima. Integer losses round up per
the Basic Set's general rounding convention. Injury and fatigue records remain
attached. A checkpoint returns an unconscious, dead or fatigue-collapsed active
form to the native approved build, preserving the disabling state. Morph pricing
is 100 points plus the positive template cost difference; Alternate Form retains
its distinct 90% premium calculation.

Voluntary reversion also takes ten seconds: the target build remains active until
the deadline, and interrupting reversion keeps it active. An authored, reasonably
common external influence names the native-form override; only director authority
may submit its matching forced-reversion command. Injury and active defense during
pending transformation, reversion or memorization produce a recorded Will-3 check
under Campaigns fourth printing B366. Failed checks interrupt concentration;
unconscious actors cannot begin or complete it.

Core Morph requires an authored existing living or formerly living model. The
model must be present and visible, or the form must have been memorized. After
assuming a form, memorization takes a minute of concentration. The current IQ
limits memory slots; a full memory requires explicitly choosing which form to
overwrite. Core equipment remains owned by the same actor. Authored compatible
items retain readiness; other equipment is unequipped and unready. Reversion does
not automatically ready equipment that has been put aside.

Focused fixtures exercise compiled ST, HP loss, equipment state, interruption,
knockout, ten-second reversion, minute-long memorization, IQ capacity, visibility,
authority, retries, stale revisions and persisted stream-fold equality. Modifiers
and supplemental form variants remain rejected and unverified. Certification rows
are unchanged: these fixtures alone do not establish whole-product compliance.
#758 and #759 remain separate follow-on work.
