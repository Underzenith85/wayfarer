# Bounded Wither Limb through a manufactured Staff

The source entry on B244–245 specifies a Melee spell resisted by HT, a fixed gross cost of 5 FP, permanent crippling and a separate 1d injury. B242 supplies the default one second of concentration; learning requires Magery 2 and Paralyze Limb with its lawful learning chain. The first consumer uses an actual manufactured Staff, approved source-bound learning, healthy normal-mana noncombat casting and an ordinary living human arm target with maximum HP at least 10. The maximum-HP boundary avoids unresolved small-body limb-damage caps; it does not require an uninjured victim at delivery.

Start accepts the charge; a registered Concentrate advances one real second; completion rolls spell skill and pays the existing skill-adjusted FP cost. At skill 16 ordinary success costs 4 FP, ordinary failure 1, critical success 0 and critical failure 4 plus canonical backfire. Waiting alone never credits casting work. Holding adds no energy cost. Other spell casting while holding is refused.

An actual Staff attack binds a private contact to the real pending defense. Current victim and productive carrier legality are checked before physical dice. The final resolved physical location determines the spell target. Successful Dodge or weapon Parry retains the charge; an actual nonlimb hit consumes it without manufacturing a limb, resistance roll or magical damage. An initially living victim killed by the same physical blow does not invalidate that blow. Hand delivery, combat casting, legs/extremities, random location, joint-grip delivery and limb-specific Block/barehand contact are explicit unsupported variants.

B240 requires an independent second spell roll at resisted Melee contact. Ordinary success contests current HT with the Rule of 16 and defender ties; contact critical success bypasses HT. Contact critical failure uses the canonical normal-mana spell backfire with a distinct pending identity and no second FP payment. This critical-failure treatment composes the ordinary spell critical rules with Melee contact; the initial charge's critical success does not bypass the second contact roll.

A successful arm effect inflicts the independent 1d HP packet and imposes a true permanent cripple even when the die is 1 and below the ordinary limb threshold. The independently reviewed composed B421 policy treats Wither's real injury and guaranteed cripple as one magical major wound: one separate magical knockdown/stunning HT check is required even on the low die. The physical Staff packet retains its own checks. No hit-location, physical critical, Strong attack or mastery multiplier enters the magical packet. The ordinary-human admission excludes unsupported injury transformations; the locationless x1 packet is not an Injury Tolerance exemption.

Permanent crippling has genuine arm/drop/two-hand retention/grip/shield/action consequences. It does not enter the ordinary random crippling-duration procedure. Natural elapsed time, restored HP and ordinary Great Healing do not cure it. B248 Great Healing expressly preserves crippled body parts; a source-qualified magical limb restoration remains unsupported until its actual consumer exists.

The producer has its own private `wither-spell` operation, strict generation 3 and `wither-spell:` ledger. Existing Deathtouch generation 1 and Paralyze generation 2 retain their interpretation. Complete contact/injury checks remain canonical and private. Caster findings contain apparent outcome, target limb, status and drops; they exclude opposed ratings, HP and private physical/mana proofs. Victims receive no private charge finding from failed or held contacts.

This bounded arm producer does not close whole Body Control issue #779 or claim a magical cure, broader anatomy or specialized delivery route. Actual producer/contact tests and separately labeled health-reducer diagnostics establish different boundaries and must not be counted interchangeably.
