# Ranged target posture: bounded B551 consumer

The selected Campaigns fourth-printing B551 Target column gives −2 to hit a
crouching, kneeling or sitting defender's torso, groin or legs with a ranged
attack. The attacker does not receive a melee posture penalty for shooting
from a lowered posture. These are distinct from active-defense penalties.

The new consumer applies the literal target-location rule once in ordinary
ranged attack specifications, including actual thrown weapons, paid Fireball
release and ordinary composed Innate Attack delivery. An omitted ordinary
body location retains the existing torso interpretation. Other admitted visible
locations keep their native location modifiers. Carried-object targets, ground
points/area aim and Maledictions use their existing distinct delivery contracts
and do not acquire this body-target penalty.

Spraying fire validates every declared recipient with the same canonical
specification before original or response-option dice, including the existing
voluntary minimum. Its live next-recipient transition and pure projection share
one function, and later recipients inherit the initiating rule policy. If a
fresh condition makes a later attack unsupported after an earlier shot has
committed, the ordinary pending continuation and attacker abandonment preserve
the earlier roll, injury and spent ammunition. No late revalidation throws away
that earlier entropy. Actual drowsy exposure tests this boundary before the first
recipient and after the first recipient has already taken damage.

Suppression is a canonical zone delivery with its own exposure/random-location
contract. This ordinary aimed-body policy is not bound retroactively when an
actor later enters a zone. Its native B409 behavior remains a separate open
source consumer; this correction does not expand suppression or area rules.

The private `ranged_target_posture_generation: 1` input field is independent of
Symptoms, defense posture and the ordinary combat feature list. It retains
byte-exact original intent and is never accepted as a player-selected modifier.
The actual launch pins its policy in a private `combat-ranged-target-policy:`
event. Ordinary weapons use their actual equipment profile; missiles and
composed sources use their actual Basic Set attack profile independently of
ordinary equipment feature dispatch. Lite and prototype outcomes remain native.
The receipt requires the exact integer generation, one matching event and its
actual actor owner. Genesis cannot supply this reserved event, and player
commands and audience projections do not expose a policy selector.

Wait captures its initiating policy before a fresh trigger, and pending attacks
retain their launch policy when a fresh defender uses a different generation.
Only policy is pinned: current target posture is read before the first original
roll. The existing response-local canonical specification retains that target
through response-option dice and mandatory health consequences. An original
roll, Luck reroll or owner-damage acceptance keeps its captured context; it does
not rescore with a later feature setting or add the posture penalty twice.

For opted-in composed attacks and Fireballs, the B345 voluntary minimum uses
the final canonical specification, including current DX condition modifiers.
Composed declaration and original/defense stages validate before new dice;
paid missile release validates before health or original-attack dice. A
previously paid charge remains held when a proposed release is refused. Actual
DX10 default6, sitting target−2 and Drowsy−2 produce an illegal final2;
Tipsy−1 instead produces lawful final3. Paid Fireball eye shots likewise
exercise literal final1/final2 refusals and final3 admission. Historical absent
policy and already captured originals retain their recorded targets, including
old targets below three. No global defense or Malediction floor is added.

The source's crawling/lying footnote needs attacker height, relative elevation,
range and sometimes head-down visibility. Construction has an optional physical
height noun, but there is no approved construction-height join to current combat
actors, and no canonical head-down producer. This implementation does not invent
height, altitude anchors, concealment or a free head movement. Requests needing
those unresolved exposure facts refuse before new attack/response entropy or
costs. Already committed turns, charged spells and recorded originals remain
intact. Arms, hands and skull receive no invented universal penalty; ordinary
targetability and location admission still apply.

Random locations against a lowered target and the mapping of the named vitals
location to this table remain explicitly unsupported pending source review.
The source rule is not silently applied retroactively after a random hit location
is rolled. This is bounded support for the clear crouch/kneel/sit target term,
not completion of all B551 exposure, body footprints, location visibility or
the whole B364 Change Posture section.

The existing separate owner-damage pause cannot preserve a thrown weapon's
ready-item invariant after the throw removes it from inventory. Newly opted-in
thrown attacks therefore refuse that staged route before original/choice dice;
ordinary immediate thrown settlement and typed opponent-original choices work.
An actual armed-grenade rethrow with a canonical warhead and the target's plain
`none` defense is different: payload settlement has no physical or shield damage
stage, so it completes without opening an owner-damage pause. This bounded route
requires the actual ready source and its unique, same-encounter, unexpired armed
cause. The existing grenade-fuse generation independently determines whether the
same cause is relocated; historical generation behavior remains unchanged.
The refusal still precedes a staged original when owner damage is requested at
BeginOpponentAttack, where no actual defense choice is available. Other defense
choices and unarmed or invalid grenade causes do not gain the exception, so no
fresh Luck entropy is rolled and then discarded. Historical absent posture
policy retains its historical route behavior. No generic thrown pause or
item-custody support is added here.

Evidence uses actual Change Posture/free crouch, approved builds, loaded guns,
real throws, paid missile charges and bound composed sources. On both stores,
the same controlled seed rolls 12 against native13 or source11: the former
inflicts 3HP and the latter misses. Corresponding composed/missile cases roll
11 against native12 or source10, also distinguishing injury from a miss. Launch
and response reexecute from their recorded seeds after fresh settings change.
Other cases exercise every literal location, exact source1/source3 attack
boundaries, real Wait movement triggers, purchased Luck rerolls, owner damage,
authority/stale refusal, unchanged area/object/Malediction delivery and explicit
unsupported lanes before entropy. No source status or certification is promoted.
