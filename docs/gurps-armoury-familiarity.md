# Armoury familiarity observations

This bounded extension to #818 uses the supplied Characters third-printing B178
Armoury unfamiliarity rule and B169 familiarity distinction. A trusted seated GM
can record an unfamiliar model, a known model, or a model the GM considers similar
to a known one. Equipment definition, restoration specialty and performer come
from current canonical equipment and the approved specialty/TL purchase. Players
cannot author the observation or supply a numeric skill bonus.

The next source-bound melee-weapon or body-armor repair captures the observed
modifier in its accepted skill: unfamiliar models incur -2, while known or
GM-adjudicated similar models incur zero. This adds to the existing TL, tool and
repair-difficulty calculation; it does not replace those modifiers. Later GM
observations affect subsequent repairs and preserve an already accepted task's
captured skill, materials and deadline. The ordinary repair transaction owns the
actual object HP, failure, conservation and replay consequences.

The private service uses the canonical CAS, recorded command and authority
pipeline. Observations use exact model definitions and specialties, do not advance
time, and spend no dice or materials. Current GM membership is checked on exact
retry. The private family and its receipts remain outside public command schemas.

Missing observations retain historical zero adjustment. This preserves prior
commands and does not establish familiarity for a fresh unknown model. The
supported subset requires an explicit GM observation; no global Armoury
certification follows. Automatic eight-hour learning, initial familiarity-count
allocation, six-familiarity similarity rolls, additional specialties, new workshop
and tool variants, and alternative repair-time policies remain unsupported here.
