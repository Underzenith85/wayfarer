# Haste manufacture followed by Power installation

`test_haste_manufacture_power_composition.py` starts with an owned mundane wearable: no enchantments, HasteItem, Power ledger or project. Its approved-build fixture includes Enchant, Haste, Power and the source prerequisites. Both recipes are pinned before campaign genesis. Real EnchantmentService Create/Begin/Advance/Settle commands manufacture Haste1, then install Power1 or Power2 on that same completed item. No direct reducer or predeclared Power substitutes for either producer.

B480–482 supplies Haste's 250 energy per level, Power's 500/1,000 energy for one/two points, and the lower Enchant/specific-spell skill. Two mages perform 125 Haste workdays followed by 250/500 Power workdays. B481 forbids a second eight-hour shift in the same day; the next project starts its credited shift the next day. Slow-and-sure settlement neither spends nor restores FP/HP merely because shared time passed.

The manufactured item's Power (skill) is 17. B482 item skill does not reduce its spell cost or casting time: the actual pre-Power Haste1 cast takes two seconds, costs two FP and changes Move5→6/Dodge8→9. Canceling that cast costs another FP. Power1 then reduces a new item cast to one FP and upkeep to zero; the awake effect persists through 121 seconds without further FP cost. Power2 makes the worn item optional always-on with no casting FP cost; actual off/on commands remove and restore those scores. The item's enchantment skill and its energy-reduction amount remain distinct.

Source-distinct failure cases inspect physical state. Ordinary first Haste manufacture failure destroys the mundane target and prevents a subsequent Power project. Ordinary added-Power failure preserves the newly manufactured Haste at its full two-FP casting cost. Critical Power failure destroys the entire item, including its existing Haste enchantment. Each campaign folds and seed-reexecutes from its original authored genesis, including every private admission command, on SQLite and PostgreSQL.

The same successful campaigns prove unauthorized/stale settlement refusal, late checkpoint rollback, restart and exact retry, changed-retry refusal without campaign/history/stream mutation, and owner/other-player/spectator campaign and stream projections. Replay compares every campaign field, normalizing only JSON object-key ordering inside `play_json`; all keys, values and list order remain significant. Stored retries, history and events retain exact byte equality. This follows the canonical state contract and does not change production serialization or historical commands.

This is two-producer composition evidence for [#785](https://github.com/Underzenith85/wayfarer/issues/785), not whole-issue closure or source certification. Unknown found-item discovery/Analyze Magic, critical hidden enhancement, duplicate Power composition, other Staff/enchantment consumers and unsupported carrier variants remain unverified. Existing separate source and construction evidence is not replaced by this test.
