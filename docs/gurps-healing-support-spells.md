# Healing support spells (Characters third printing B248)

Lend Energy and Lend Vitality use the existing approved spell channel, casting clock, deterministic checks and resource payment. Their selected energy cost is never reduced by high skill. Lend Energy restores actual eligible patient FP up to the canonical maximum, without erasing fatigue whose recovery requires food, water or sleep (Campaigns fourth printing B426). It ends immediately.

Lend Vitality restores temporary patient HP for one hour, cannot be maintained, and expires on the actual resource engine clock. Borrowed HP is recorded independently of wounds and permanent healing; expiry removes the temporary amount without inventing a fresh wound or clearing injury facts. Per B237, only the most powerful active instance counts. A weaker recast remains available after a stronger instance expires; the amounts never stack. Early cancellation pays the shared B237 energy cost and removes its contribution. Critical failures use the existing authored patient-harm gate from B248.

Recover Energy is passive. Its Hard learning definition requires Magery 1 and purchased Lend Energy. The authoritative medical rest service derives the exact approved skill and requires an explicitly authored normal/high/very-high mana environment. Skill 15 recovers one FP per 300 seconds; skill 20 recovers one per 120 seconds, with no further improvement. It makes no casting roll and affects only the resting character. It cannot accelerate recovery in low/no mana or while concentrating. Ordinary maintained spells remain allowed. Existing restricted-fatigue entitlements, rest interruption, CAS and receipts remain in force; faster intervals apply to magical-power FP as well as ordinary FP.

Evidence: `tests/test_healing_support_effects.py` validates construction and observes actual transferred FP, hour expiry on the shared clock, most-powerful temporary HP, failed maintenance, paid cancellation, and persisted approved-build MedicalService rest at the two thresholds and invalid mana levels. No Awaken expansion is included; it remains owned by #774. Overall compliance gates remain separate from these three concrete spell effects.
