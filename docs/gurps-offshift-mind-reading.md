# Off-shift Mind Reading

Fresh exact-Basic campaigns admit the existing native Mind Reading activation and resolution between daily enchanting work shifts. Characters B69 supplies one second of concentration and the native resisted reading attempt; maintained successful reading needs no further concentration. Campaigns B481 supplies an eight-hour workday. This implementation adds no nightly quiet-rest requirement or enchanting FP-credit ledger.

Activation checks the complete actual one-second interval before turn-start health dice, exertion or FP costs. It preserves the native concentration effect, actor availability, target/channel/build checks, Rule of 16 and real recovery-task interruption. Work-active intervals refuse. Completion exactly at the following shift boundary is permitted only for the authenticated pending operation; ongoing nonconcentrating effects acquire no new work restriction.

The private `ability_offshift_generation: 1` is independent of Rule16. A private `ability-offshift:` resource event binds the actual native producer command and complete concentrating effect. It is read against that producer and the current concentration episode. Cancellation and a new activation at the identical timestamp cannot make an unmarked producer inherit the old policy. Unknown generations, altered effects and swapped intervals fail closed. Historical absence preserves original command bytes, raw delegated-principal grammar and unmarked producer behavior; a fresh responder never upgrades an old producer.

A shared pending boundary runs before pipeline assessment and again under the campaign lock before reduction. It initially admits only matching native resolve/cancel, ordinary Wait, and AdvanceEnchanting. Full native Wait/Advance intervals must end no later than the captured one-second readiness; another actor cannot bypass the concentrating mage's work boundary. At readiness the table resolves or cancels before advancing farther. This is a bounded supported continuation, not a claim that B69 forces an immediate roll. Native cancellation retains its existing authoritative channel requirements.

Unqualified commands refuse before native effects, dice or cost. This includes physical, scene, noncombat, social-media, party, combat and other spell/ability time hosts, whose durations can otherwise be discovered after entropy. Fresh trusted source callbacks outside the pipeline are also fenced before invocation in Physical, Social, Medical rest, DrowsyToxins, Survival and Drinking. Stored retries reuse their existing receipts/source captures. All other native persisted mutations enter the same pipeline; catalog writes address a separate scenario catalog and do not change live play or game time. No reducer is run speculatively and no late clock rollback discards dice.

The B345 source checkpoint applies only to an enrolled producer's actual voluntary resolve attempt. Its canonical native B69 attacker target is shared with the actual resistance roll, including current condition modifiers and repeat/range penalties. Effective target 2 refuses before distraction/contest dice, retains the pending concentration and already-spent activation, and permits cancellation; target 3 rolls normally. Activation itself makes no success roll and is not rejected merely for a possible later target below 3. Historical producers and mandatory survival/health rolls are unchanged. Existing original/Luck records remain subject to their independent pending boundary.

`tests/test_ability_offshift.py` exercises real authored Mind Reading and Slow-and-Sure projects, both stores, actual one-second Wait/Advance, exact next-shift completion, refusal with untouched RNG, source controls, retries, strict lineage, the named B345 target 2/3 checkpoint, and original-genesis replay for both fresh and raw legacy delegated principals (including a colon in the principal).

Off-shift analyze, maintenance, target switching, other ability families, grouped clocks and active combat remain outside this extension. Existing maintenance behavior is not widened. Real FP recovery remains the existing Medical/recovery ledger; nothing here asserts a general prohibition on nighttime psi or zero-FP use.
