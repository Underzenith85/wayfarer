The package pins their identities, source pages, IQ/Hard learning shape, and family selection. Runtime attempts use the shared authority, targeting, interruption, privacy, replay, and restart boundary. Unsupported effect variants remain explicit boundaries. The selected-printing construction metadata is reviewed. Runtime coverage follows the concrete consumers below.

Their bounded host tests do not promote the whole catalog to runtime completeness. Its separate magical HP packet, current resistance, permanent condition and original-genesis replay are actual consumer evidence; qualified magical limb restoration and broader delivery/anatomy variants remain unverified. Source inventory rows stay partial under [Rooted compositions #1018](https://github.com/Underzenith85/wayfarer/issues/1018) and [Melee delivery and limb variants #1019](https://github.com/Underzenith85/wayfarer/issues/1019) for the explicitly unverified variants.
