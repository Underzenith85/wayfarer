# Bounded Analyze Magic execution

Analyze Magic now has an approved construction chain and an actual private host for a single completed critical Haste binding. Detect Magic, Identify Spell and Analyze Magic use their printed IQ/Hard learning requirements. The execution subset requires skill 10–14, normal mana, a currently carried caster-owned item, and trusted GM confirmation of touching, familiarity and absence of concealment. Multiple bindings and other unverified subjects are refused before dice.

Start records the accepted item, binding, approved build and physical observation. Work records an explicit hour of casting with canonical clock checkpoints; unrelated elapsed time cannot complete it. A pending caster cannot perform another action, while other actors remain free. Cancellation releases the commitment. Injury or distraction requires the printed Will−3 continuation check, including after the hour and before completion. Timed and combat injury carriers are currently refused rather than inferred as uninterrupted work. Canonical injury/restore checkpoint diagnostics can verify retained distraction and the real continuation command, but do not constitute an instantaneous health-service execution proof.

The completion roll retains its accepted base skill and applies canonical current IQ condition and symptom modifiers plus current injury shock. Will continuation applies canonical current Will conditions. Existing spells on are refused in this first subset rather than given an unverified maintenance count. Checkpoint injury diagnostics establish immediate ready-phase shock behavior, not a new temporal injury/healing service or shock-expiry policy. Further Work refuses current injury shock before dice or clock advancement: this carrier has no canonical injury-turn hook proving when shock expires, so casting-stage temporal injury continuation remains unsupported. Ready completion still applies the current shock penalty.

The information roll is secret and costs the full 8 FP for success, ordinary failure and critical failure. The initial single-binding subset enforces one attempt per caster per game day. A trusted GM publishes the actual finding on success, nothing on ordinary failure, and an explicitly authored incorrect rating on critical failure. No invented random false-information formula is used.

Observer knowledge is bound to the exact item, project, binding and canonical rating. A false finding is an apparent belief, never certified knowledge. Player inventory and findings use the same presentation for true and false reports; other actors and spectators do not gain knowledge, and transferring ownership does not transfer discovery. Canonical enchantment rating, energy discount, secret check and replay history remain unchanged. Existing critical item casting diagnostics remain privately projected by the separate receipt privacy boundary.

Actual host tests start from original mundane-item genesis, manufacture Haste through EnchantmentService, transfer the item, declare the physical observation, perform the casting work, roll and publish the report. SQLite and PostgreSQL cases cover all three outcomes, authority, fixed retry, restart, CAS rollback, daily refusal, unrelated clock cancellation, registered commands and full seeded command replay. This bounded consumer does not certify Analyze Magic progression through multiple enchantments, unknown spells, concealment resistance, found-item provenance, other mana levels, ceremony, or every information spell.
