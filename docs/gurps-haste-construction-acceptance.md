# Haste construction and personal-cast acceptance

Basic Set B235 makes each spell a separate learned skill with no default; ordinary spells use IQ/Hard unless marked otherwise. B251 marks Haste as a Regular spell without a prerequisite requirement. Its construction must therefore not invent a minimum Magery prerequisite. Normal-mana casting eligibility remains separate from permission to learn the skill.

The construction oracle compiles the actual movement-package Haste purchase at four points with IQ 12 and Magery 2. The source-derived learned level is 14. The build spends 69 points: IQ improvement 40, Magery 0 five, two further Magery levels 20, and Haste four. The same complete purchase against a GM budget of 65 must produce budget.overspent and no validated build. This is an invalid character construction, not an invalid runtime Haste magnitude and not a new spell prerequisite. B10 governs the GM character-point budget; B170 governs the IQ/Hard skill cost and B66 the Magery purchase.

The companion personal-cast test starts with the existing approved-build fixture, executes Haste through its real service, and verifies Move 5→8 and Dodge 8→11, six FP paid, two seconds casting, and return to baseline at real time 62. Unauthorized control, stale revision and changed exact-retry bytes refuse atomically. Reloaded exact retries retain the accepted result. The full personal casting and expiry history folds and seed-reexecutes on SQLite and PostgreSQL, with canonical final campaign equality.

This supplies the previously missing explicit invalid Haste construction evidence in #797 and an independently checked personal-Haste replay path. Existing tests cover ordinary failure, high skill, strongest-effect behavior, positive-SM costs, wearer custody and item life cycles. It does not implement or certify Great Haste self mid-turn activation, combined named/Step casting, split/after casting Steps, cross-encounter targeting, or the unsupported Apportation variants. These unverified carrier extensions are owned by [the remaining Movement host carriers (#980)](https://github.com/Underzenith85/wayfarer/issues/980). The written #797 baseline is reviewed through the case map below; publication and closure still require the independent review and exact-head CI.
