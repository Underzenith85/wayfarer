# Cinematic strength consumers

Selected sources: Characters third printing B182/B201/B215/B216 and Campaigns fourth printing B426-427.

The source requires Trained By A Master for Breaking Blow and Push; Weapon Master alone no longer satisfies those prerequisites. Power Blow may be learned with either mastery advantage. Weapon Master activation requires its approved muscle-powered melee weapon and mode; a different weapon does not receive the effect.

Cinematic skill FP costs use canonical continued-exertion and fatigue handling. Failed exertion persists collapse without rolling the ability. Attempt costs apply on success and failure, are recorded as power fatigue, and update actual fatigue thresholds. Exact retries neither reroll nor spend again. The enclosing command commits one resource revision despite its internal health effects.

Instantaneous Power Blow uses the source -10 modifier. Triple ST requires skill above 20 and another -10. The successful effect binds to one specific attack command, encounter, turn and approved build. Existing armed/unarmed damage consumers use the resulting ST table; other commands and intervening turns or clock advancement receive no benefit. Failed attempts also reserve their bound attack, preventing repeated preparation rolls for the same attack. An authored momentary lift uses the same power roll and changes the existing lifting capacity procedure, with ordinary Lifting bonuses still applied. Neither activation permanently changes the approved character.

This is incremental #806 engine work. Verified timed concentration and Breaking Blow armor/brittleness and critical self-injury still require implementation. The bounded Push/Immovable Stance displacement consumer is documented in [cinematic displacement](gurps-cinematic-displacement.md); combined Power Blow/Push strength remains unsupported. No rule certification is promoted; end-to-end API/UI gates remain open. Tests exercise actual armed/unarmed injury and authored lift results, exclusions, failed costs, fatigue thresholds, failed exertion, authority, prerequisite rejection and durable retries.
