# Complete-source purification boundary

Characters third printing B253, physical PDF page 255, permits Purify Water to
remove impurities from water flowing through a ring/fingers into a container.
It costs 1 per gallon; the supported ordinary stream uses 5–10 seconds per gallon.

The source-clear case is draining the entire actual heterogeneous water source.
For a source with 3 gallons, 2 of them recorded pure, purifying all 3 leaves
exactly zero water and zero pure water in that source and supplies 3 pure gallons
to an empty clean receiver. No assumption about which parcel flows first is
needed. Partial withdrawals still lack a supported composition/order record.

A real approved 3-gallon casting on the pre-change engine schedules 15 seconds
and rolls successfully, then rejects this legitimate full transfer as an
unsupported mixed-source composition. That red oracle observes the real
campaign host, not just a helper or catalog flag.

The private `purify_entire_source` intent is absent/false for historical
commands, preserving their exact commitment bytes and mixed-source restriction.
For opted-in commands, the current source must still contain exactly the
committed quantity at admission and completion, before dice or material changes.
The immutable per-cast plan prevents switching source or quantity. Full successful
purification empties all source parcels; failure moves none. Energy, ordinary
casting time, authority, capacity and receiver recontamination retain their
existing source-derived owners.

This bounded change does not define fractional-gallon energy rounding, faster
large-ring rates, partial heterogeneous withdrawals, dilution thresholds,
contaminant concentration, toxin/health effects or other unsupported variants.
The authorized narrow join changes only the private WaterPlan and its material
admission/application. Sixteen real host cases across SQLite and PostgreSQL
prove success/failure, independent energy/time/material expectations, clean and
impure receiving-vessel interactions, restart, exact retry and stale revision,
all-command seeded re-execution, and rejection after a second approved caster
actually increases the source volume at the first cast's completion deadline.
That changed-source rejection draws no dice, pays no energy and changes no
material. Partial-source intents still fail at admission. Domain and legacy
checks preserve original cast-plan bytes and all five public spell schemas.
This is not a full issue 804 acceptance claim.
