# Held Staff contact through ordinary melee

The bounded consumer attaches a private held Deathtouch charge to the actual
ordinary Staff attack's pending identity. It uses an actually manufactured Staff,
source-approved spell learning, paid one-second casting, current custody and an
ordinary human target. Admission occurs before attack dice and again before
physical settlement dice. Current sensory and physical defense restrictions remain
in force. The public missile-spell field is not reused.

A hit settles the normal Staff blow and a separate selected 1–3d magical HP packet
in the same transaction. Successful Block or barehanded Parry still makes magical
contact; Dodge or a weapon Parry retains the charge. The physical public injury
receipt describes its own packet. The private magical receipt records its own
HP before/after; final actor health and encounter readiness reflect both packets.

B240's ordinary blow plus separate spell effect, B245's selected damage, and
B556's critical blow table are composed here: maximum/doubled physical damage
does not multiply the spell dice. This is a documented interpretation of those
rules, rather than a printed sentence expressly exempting spell damage.

Barehanded Parry is admitted only for this durable charged Staff association.
B376's weapon penalty applies before selecting the best skill; thrusts and
Judo/Karate preserve their exceptions. A hand does not select weapon-breakage
mechanics. B557 closed consequences reuse the ordinary unarmed consequence
engine. Contextual unconsciousness/tear results preserve the table, hand,
checks, effect dice and pending identity in a private record and pause. The
armed critical continuation command does not resolve those contextual outcomes.

The contact matrix uses original mundane genesis, real Staff manufacture, held
casting, an actual pending attack, current authority/stale refusals, both SQLite
and PostgreSQL, exact retries after restart, and a separately seeded full command
reexecution. Numeric scripted-dice cases are distinct from the seeded proof.
Reexecution compares all values and list order, allowing only JSON object key
ordering; retry and persisted history remain exact.

Remaining boundaries include hand delivery, transformed/nonhuman targets,
heavy barehanded-parry knockback, contextual B557 continuation and owner Luck
damage staging. This bounded consumer does not close the whole Melee/Staff issue.

The expanded named host suite is `tests/test_staff_melee_contact.py`. It also
proves physical maximum, double and triple outcomes leave magical dice unchanged,
including a lethal physical blow; leather torso DR applies to the physical packet
while the magical packet ignores it. A fresh critical bare Parry can disable the
caster's charging arm after contact was validated and still settle the spell.
Magic finish checks the accepted charge/event identity. Same-transaction physical
consequences cannot retroactively negate that contact; later or deferred commands
revalidate current carrier and target. Prior source loss fails before response
dice. The validation proof is an ephemeral function keyword, never serialized.

Readiness/touch tests are explicitly canonical reducer diagnostics: a genuine
held Staff may be unready without losing its hand binding, while actual unequip
and reconciliation dissipate the charge permanently even after re-equipping.
Those diagnostics do not assert a public movement/drop/retrieval producer while
an attack is pending. Only actual private held Staff identities receive that
projection exception; ordinary historical inventory behavior is unchanged.

The labeled legacy-limb-adapter diagnostic exercises real public
`ContinueCriticalMiss` migration/resumption after immediate spell discharge.
The independent continuation handler resolves the physical context without
repeating magical dice or injury. Fresh complete Staff contexts resolve that
armed row directly; the diagnostic does not claim a natural fresh pause. B557
contextual bare-limb pauses remain a separate unsupported continuation boundary.
