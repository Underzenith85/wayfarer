# Healing spells (Characters third printing B248)

Minor Healing, Major Healing and Great Healing now use source-reviewed learning definitions and the authoritative shared spell reducer. Their approved spell channels restore canonical patient HP through `restore_hp`; casting results record the actual amount restored. They cannot revive the dead, clear disease or poison, or repair missing/crippled body parts.

Minor restores one HP per selected energy (standard cap 3); Major restores two (standard cap 4 energy). B237 Magery and Effect raises these finite energy caps to Magery when higher. Great costs 20 energy, takes one minute (B249), and restores all eligible missing HP. The shared reducer retains high-skill cost reductions, critical success, failure energy costs, HP contributions and concentration deadlines. Healing is permanent and immediately ends its cast rather than contributing an ongoing-spell penalty.

Completed attempts persist in spell events. Minor and Major independently impose -3 per previous attempt by the same caster on the same patient in the current shared-clock day. Great permits only one try per patient per day regardless of caster, including a failed roll (B249). Self-casting subtracts missing HP from the casting skill. Day boundaries use the engine's established 86400-second calendar.

Physician 15+ is derived from the approved, pinned canonical Physician definition and compiled skill. It changes a first Minor/Major critical failure into ordinary failure, but not a repeat attempt. Great has the source Physician exception. An unmitigated critical failure records a pending patient-harm consequence instead of rolling the unrelated B236 caster table. A campaign-authored row-0 damage alternative must injure exactly the original patient; the existing backfire resolver records the chosen injury. It cannot waive or retarget that harm. Source B248 gives examples of harm but no fixed damage formula, so the engine deliberately requires the authored interpretation and blocks further play until resolved.

Evidence: `tests/test_healing_spell_effects.py` follows approved construction through actual HP restoration, energy payment, permanent completion, source energy caps, distinct repeat penalties, Great daily benefit, critical-failure persistence/retry, authored patient injury, and approved Physician mitigation. Existing shared spell/lifecycle/backfire tests preserve authority and deterministic replay. Frozen player v1 operations stay unchanged; the engine event schema gains additive healing spell identities and actual HP-restored evidence.

This closes the concrete three-spell effect boundary in #772. The merged #752 canonical recovery boundary permits magical healing for both Partial and Total Unhealing (B160); healing spells use that boundary. Overall source and end-to-end compliance gates remain separate.
