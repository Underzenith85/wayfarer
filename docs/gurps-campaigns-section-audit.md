## Executable joins

The ledger records the exact independent test files on each promoted row. The
joins cover success rolls and contests; supported physical and sensory checks;
influence, Will, and Fright Checks; special movement and ranged procedures;
afflictions; vehicles; invention and enchanting projects; object damage and
repair; and reusable appendix tables for range, hit locations, critical results,
object protection, and reactions.

An indexed table that only summarizes rules proved at their owning sections is
classified as `construction-reference-data`. GM advice, adventure design, and
world-building prompts are `reference-only-guidance`. Neither classification is
an implementation claim.

## Appendix profile treatment

The 12 former `setting-unresolved` appendix rows are generic Basic Set material,
not Infinite Worlds setting content. Six are reusable executable mechanics with
independent evidence: the Size and Speed/Range table, hit-location tables,
critical tables, object/cover protection, NPC reactions, and the Reaction Table.
The other six are structural or reference summaries: the Tables heading, combat
modifier summary, maneuver and posture summaries, Campaign Planning Form, and
Time Use Sheet. The existing B523-B546 Infinite Worlds exclusion remains intact.
