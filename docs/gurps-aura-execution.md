# Aura: bounded living-human information

The private Aura command family implements the Characters B249 Information spell from an approved purchased IQ/Hard definition requiring learned Detect Magic. Purchased skill 10–14 in authenticated normal mana gives one explicit second of casting and a full 3 FP cost on every rolled outcome. The cost does not scale with subject size. The initial nonceremonial subset admits one attempt per caster per game day and current ordinary speech/gesture rituals; it does not certify every Information or high-skill variant.

A trusted GM supplies explicitly complete living-human classification, personality, current violent emotion and secret-fact observations for a physically touching co-located actor. These facts are qualitative adjudicated world knowledge, not mechanics or player-authored spell truth. The source supplies no numeric personality insight tiers: after seeing the secret check, the GM authors the appropriate personality insight without an invented margin-to-description formula. Approximate mage wording must agree with current actual purchased Magery, including Magery 0; it never supplies an effective spell skill. Current canonical influence and possession grants supply control truth.

The observation binds the current entity, human body, approved build, actor conditions, subject world facts and canonical control grants. Unknown or dead current injury state, machine/unliving physiology and displaced possessing consciousness refuse admission. A newer authenticated observation supersedes older physical-subject facts. Current witnesses are rechecked before dice and after the casting second. Changed location, current subject facts, authority, party activity or availability refuses atomically. The first clock subset excludes active encounters, timed hazards, existing caster spells on and temporal injury continuation.

Purchased traits are not automatically secret. A GM may mark a real positively purchased trait by definition ID and provide its secret description; unsupported references refuse. Narrative world secrets carry no purchased mechanics. Ordinary success derives mage, control and violent-emotion findings from the accepted snapshot without revealing marked secrets. Critical success automatically includes the complete marked secret list: a report cannot select, omit or replace those secrets. Reports retain the accepted historical truth rather than refreshing it from later ambient state.

Information checks and their outcomes stay private. Ordinary failure publishes no finding. Critical failure requires a genuinely different GM-authored apparent finding, separate from canonical truth. True and false findings have the same audience fields; unrelated players and spectators see neither private observations nor findings. Exact retries, restart, stale commands, checkpoint/commit rollback and original-genesis command folding/reexecution are exercised on SQLite and PostgreSQL.

The actual host tests prove mage and nonmage findings, violent-emotion reporting, secret-trait disclosure only on critical success, all secret outcomes, current ritual constraints, real subject movement and superseded observations. Additional canonical lethal-injury and influence/possession reducer diagnostics prove witness rejection and current control derivation. Those diagnostics are not registered injury or mental-control host producers and do not claim original-genesis replay of those separate systems.

Zombies, vampires, illusions, created beings, other anatomy, distant subjects, absent or incomplete qualitative facts, concealment, other mana, ceremonial casting, high-skill cost/time reductions and timed/combat subject changes remain unverified. No public spell ID or shared SpellResult schema is expanded. This bounded Aura slice does not certify all Knowledge-college variants or promote a whole source section.
