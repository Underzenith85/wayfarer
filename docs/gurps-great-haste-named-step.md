# Named Great Haste with a preceding Step

This bounded private consumer combines the already admitted named Regular target
and before-action Step carriers. Characters B239 permits a known subject without
sight, applying the actual range penalty and an additional unseen penalty.
Characters B236–237 and Campaigns B366/B368 govern the existing Concentrate and
before-action Step boundary; Characters B251 supplies Great Haste's three seconds,
five energy, ten-second duration and non-self expiration fatigue.

`named-step-great-haste` records generation four. The accepted known fact and target
identity are bound into the private movement lease. The canonical Step moves the
caster before the current range/visibility calculation and casting continuation.
A paused Wait preserves that lease; resume checks the accepted named source again,
uses the already adjudicated Will-3 distraction result, and settles movement and
casting once. A renamed or replaced fact cannot silently retarget an accepted lease,
including a paused first second that has not yet created the spell effect.

The command remains private. Public schemas are unchanged, and existing generation
one through three inputs and optional-field-absent lease bytes retain their prior
shape. Absent authenticated generation refuses the new command before movement or
entropy. Tests exercise both stores, actual range/unseen skill values, FP and
expiration, failure, Wait injury, source changes, current authority, stale commands,
rollback, restart, exact retry, hidden projection and seeded command reexecution.

This does not complete issue #980. After-action or split Steps, cross-encounter
subjects, canonical Apportation placement/concentration and scene transfers,
contained or transformed current mass, and higher-energy casting remain separate
source and host-carrier work. This consumer does not create those carriers or widen
world simulation policy.

The generation-four continuation now bounds ongoing ritual movement separately
from the initial Step before casting starts. B237 uses trained base spell skill,
with only low mana's reduction, for ritual requirements. Ongoing generation-four
Steps require that value to be at least 15 and translate at most one yard. The
skill 15–19 rule supplies the one-yard precedent; skill 20+ removes the ritual,
while this carrier retains the same conservative one-yard limit. Range, unseen
penalties, shock and condition-adjusted roll targets do not determine ritual
movement eligibility. Initial before-casting Steps and recorded generation-one
through-three behavior retain their existing admission. Fresh ordinary selected Steps use generation five and the same ongoing guard;
recorded generation two retains its original admission and serialized lease shape.
Generation four remains exclusive to the named Step carrier.

B236 ends the final casting turn on the spell roll. This consumer consequently
does not admit a Step after that roll. A future intermediate after-action or
split movement carrier requires a source-resolved action phase and canonical
Wait/turn continuation, rather than movement after an already advanced turn.

Canonical Great Haste channels cannot be replaced. Their mana value is an
immutable declared source, including while a Step is paused by Wait. An actual
pending-Wait test attempts a trusted GM replacement from normal to low mana;
the host refuses before entropy or persistence. Restart then resumes the original
channel once, with unchanged casting payment and exact retry/clock replay.
Consequently there is no supported mid-lease channel-mana mutation to refresh.

Fresh generation-four/five carriers also reject an initial translation longer
than one yard, even when the approved caster's ordinary Step allowance is two
yards. This is the explicitly bounded supported carrier, not a claim that B368
forbids all larger initial movement. Larger, after-action and split casting Steps
remain unimplemented under #980. Initial one-yard Steps at base skill 10–14 remain
admitted; recorded generation-two movement remains unchanged.
