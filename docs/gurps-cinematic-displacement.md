# Knockback and Immovable Stance

The selected Campaigns fourth printing B378 rule now drives crushing damage against shields: basic damage produces whole yards from the resisting defender's ST, with the source low-ST minimum. Cutting knockback requires no penetration. An unconscious or nonresisting defender uses HP instead. The resisting defender's canonical fatigue projection applies.

The shared displacement consumer moves the actual battlefield placement and checks the entire occupied hex footprint. Balance uses the highest approved DX, Acrobatics, or Judo, with the source penalty for yards after the first. Failed balance sets the actual combat posture and injury posture prone. Perfect Balance contributes its approved source bonus.

The selected Characters third printing B201 Immovable Stance consumer rolls against the actual potential yards. Success prevents displacement; failure still permits ordinary balance; critical failure causes the full displacement and a fall. These are source-derived engine consequences, not a certification claim.

Unrepresented collision damage, map boundaries, and unknown mapless direction explicitly block the encounter for adjudication. Push activation, Stance against Judo Throw, collision damage under B430, and API/UI exposure remain work for #806 and the end-to-end compliance gates. This change does not close them.
