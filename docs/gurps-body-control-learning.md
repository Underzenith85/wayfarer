# Body Control learning construction

Printed B235/B244–245 learning metadata covers Itch, Spasm, Pain, Clumsiness,
Hinder, Rooted Feet, Paralyze Limb, Wither Limb and Deathtouch. These are purchased
IQ/Hard skills with no defaults. Hinder accepts purchased Clumsiness or Haste;
Paralyze requires Magery 1 and five other Body Control spells including Pain;
Wither requires Magery 2 and Paralyze; Deathtouch requires Wither.

The private count validator proves a topological witness among the six reviewed
lower Body Control spells. It never counts Paralyze itself or its dependent
Wither/Deathtouch purchases. Haste can qualify Hinder but is not a Body Control
count witness. Exact canonical source definitions prevent manual, missing-skill
or foreign college metadata from granting a witness. Future additional spell
definitions need source review before extending this bounded witness set.

This is construction support. It grants no runtime effects for prerequisite
spells and does not certify Staff delivery or Deathtouch execution. The Staff
enchantment itself retains its separate Enchant prerequisite and public schemas
are unchanged.
