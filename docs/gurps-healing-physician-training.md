# Physician training and the first healing attempt

Selected Characters third printing B173 requires an actual point in a skill to
receive its special benefits, independently of its numerical default. B248 gives
Physician15+ a benefit on the first Minor Healing attempt for a particular
caster/subject/day: a critical failure becomes an ordinary failure. Major Healing
uses the same rule, with its own attempt tally. An earlier successful cast also
uses up that first attempt; this is not a benefit on the first critical failure.

The approved caster binder now requires at least1 purchased point in the exact
pinned Physician definition before supplying this passive benefit. The existing
15 threshold and attempt accounting remain unchanged. A lawful Veterinary20 to
Physician15 default remains15 in the sheet and in an ordinary skill roll. Adding
one real Physician point qualifies even when its numerical15 still comes from
that default; points in other skills do not qualify. Casting remains legal
without Physician training.

Fresh typed Minor/Major Healing starts independently capture a strict private
generation and an authenticated origin bound to the first native cast event,
receipt, caster, patient, spell, build and clock. The original public intent is
retained byte for byte. Coerced/unknown generations, duplicate private keys,
changed originals and swapped/orphan origins refuse. The producer is unavailable
to authored genesis and player projections. Public command, environment, effect
and result fields are unchanged.

All typed healing starts, including combat, enter SpellService. Its explicit
execution context records the origin before the native final Concentrate can
complete. Later contexts use that origin independently of the current feature
default. No additional combat-generation flag or caster assertion is needed.
Historical absent-generation starts keep their original pending behavior and
serialized inputs. New starts retain their corrected behavior when the current
default is disabled. Pure numerical kernels do not establish another public
approved-caster producer.

The selected casting dice remain unchanged. With a2FP cast and3d18, an unpaid
Physician15 default leaves a critical failure, spends the full2FP and records
pending patient harm. Eligible Physician15 instead yields ordinary failure,
spends1FP and records no critical patient harm. No existing HP, selected roll or
energy cost is refunded or fabricated.

SQLite and PostgreSQL cases cover Minor/Major, zero/one-point and genuinely
trained Physician15, trained14, unrelated training, ordinary default15 rolls,
first-success/repetition and separate spell/caster/subject/day counters, exact
retries and original-input identity, source/legacy origin flips, private records,
authored genesis and native combat auto-completion. Original-genesis seed replay
checks folded state and exact emitted events. Final encoded state uses the
repository's documented JSON document equality; original input bytes and
whitespace checks remain exact.

Great Healing and the optional Physician12 natural-recovery bonus are separate
passive-benefit consumers. This slice does not change the frozen medical-care
candidate, ordinary Physician/Surgery rolls, default arithmetic, knowledge facts
or other spell/Luck behavior. No capability or certification status is promoted.
