# Measured finite Water collection

[Remaining Water carriers (#981)](https://github.com/Underzenith85/wayfarer/issues/981) owns the broader material and geometry contract. This implementation admits one complete mundane operation: an authenticated actor pours an entire observed homogeneous vessel into one empty, clean child of a previously observed separate-container assembly. It supports whole gallons of pure water, including actual Create Water output, or whole homogeneous impure water with an explicit current physical purity measurement.

Basic Set Characters B253 supplies permanent Create Water and purification through a ring into a receiver. It does not supply a numeric ordinary collection rate or an accelerated large-ring rate. `collection_seconds` is an authenticated scenario measurement of this complete physical pour, not a spell formula. The command executes the measured work atomically; it does not equate a later clock deadline with proof that a pour occurred.

The GM declares immutable `DeclareWaterCollection` evidence referencing current world facts: source and receiver custody, locations and positions; downhill heights; stream width and unobstructed path; aperture, clean interior, condition and capacity; and every existing receiver child’s volume and purity. Existing source material facts must be complete and agree with its canonical WaterBody. An impure source additionally requires a measured homogeneous purity fact. Inventoried or expended item aliases, nested source contents, shared receiver aliases, active prior observations, partial heterogeneous material and fractional measurements fail closed.

The controlling player executes `CollectWater` using the observation ID. Current approved build, capability, availability, custody and physical evidence are checked before canonical clock advancement and again afterward. Paused, split, queued or unsynchronized party activity, invention/enchanting commitments and active encounters are refused. Only the collector’s availability and its sole admitted party group’s readiness settle to completion; other actors’ state is preserved. Common clock settlement runs inside the same transaction, so a changed physical condition after clock advancement or a failed commit cannot leave advanced time or transferred material.

Success drains the entire source, fills the exact empty child, increases the receiver aggregate by the same quantity and pure quantity, replaces existing material facts, and advances measured time once. The operation spends no spell energy and rolls no dice. Prior parcel observations and completed flows remain immutable. A new observation and a new actual Purify Water roll are required after collection; an old successful one-roll flow cannot authorize newly collected water. Purifying collected impure water changes purity while preserving the total quantity.

Private observation and settlement records use `water-collection:`. They cannot be authored in genesis or projected as GM evidence to players or spectators. The actor receives material totals and elapsed time through the private command result. Public schemas and existing WaterPlan and historical command serialization remain unchanged. Exact retries return the original receipt, restart and checkpoint replay preserve it, and full seeded command reexecution reproduces canonical command digests and the complete parsed campaign state.

Evidence is in `test_water_collection.py`, `test_water_collection_guards.py` and `test_water_collection_replay.py`, on SQLite and PostgreSQL: actual Create → collection → fresh Purify; failed Create; homogeneous impure collection → purity-changing Purify; current-state refusals; authentic party pause/resume and split; canonical clock stale-check rollback; checkpoint and CAS failure; authority, spectator privacy, genesis admission; retry/restart and full seeded reexecution.

Unsupported #981 variants remain explicit: fractional ledgers or FP rounding; partial heterogeneous pours; dirty receivers or spills; unavailable inventory-container material adapters; natural refill from surrounding water; midair globe fall/catch geometry; continuous collection interruption; and any invented accelerated large-ring rate. No whole #981 closure is claimed.
