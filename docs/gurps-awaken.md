# Awaken: supported engine subset

Characters third printing B248 specifies Hard learning, Lend Vitality prerequisite,
Area class, base cost 1 and default one-second casting. The approved adapter derives
HT for every participant inside the authored square or hex area, never from caller
claims. Radius scales casting cost under B239; high skill reductions remain shared.

Successful casts clear canonical physical/electrical/surprise stun, mental backfire
stun and active stun afflictions. Represented sleeping/unconscious subjects receive
HT plus the caster's margin, with −3 for recorded injury unconsciousness. A failed
awakening roll preserves unconsciousness while still removing stunning. FP≤0
subjects receive no effect. The spell does not revive dead subjects or heal a mortal wound.

A successfully awakened subject below one third of basic FP owes exactly 1 FP at
the hour deadline. The shared clock rejects skipping this deadline, accrues earned
rest through the deadline, then applies the canonical magical-fatigue cost. History
and receipts preserve expiry and retry behavior without a second charge.

**Issue #774 remains partial.** Drugged unconsciousness currently rejects before
casting or dice: toxin/intoxication state does not represent awakening independently
of the continuing drug condition. Removing that drug record would also remove other
consequences and invent a cure. Legacy unconsciousness without recorded cause
also rejects rather than guessing the injury penalty. Ordinary world sleep outside
an authoritative placement channel is unsupported. Repeated overlapping alert
intervals reject pending further source-specific review before full promotion. These gaps
remain owned by #774; this change makes no end-to-end certification claim.
