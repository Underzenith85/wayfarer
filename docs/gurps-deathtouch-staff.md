# Deathtouch carried by a manufactured Staff

This private consumer joins Characters B235–237, B240, B244–245 and Campaigns B481. It is a bounded Staff consumer, not complete Melee or Body Control coverage.

A genuinely learned eight-spell route (Itch, Spasm, Pain, Clumsiness, Hinder, Paralyze Limb, Wither Limb, Deathtouch) and current approved build drives the actual cast. The fixed route is a supported subset of the broader source prerequisite alternatives. The Staff must have actual successful manufacture evidence, its exact physical construction, usable permanent binding, current custody, and a usable equipped hand. A construction observation alone grants no magic.

Casting currently supports personal skill10–24 in authenticated normal mana, a synchronous noncombat party, and healthy uninterrupted ordinary ritual. Start records the pending cast. Concentrate performs one real canonical clock second and records its work credit; Complete rolls and pays at that credited deadline. Waiting or advancing the clock does not constitute concentration. Combat casting, hand carriers, low or exceptional mana and timed hazard/distraction casting are visibly unsupported. Cancellation remains possible when the carrier becomes unusable.

Gross selected energy1–3 controls damage dice independently of FP payment. Existing high-skill energy reduction and critical-success payment apply to FP; ordinary failure pays the canonical reduced failure cost and critical failure retains canonical backfire evidence. Holding costs no extra FP or roll. Delivery must occur after the casting turn, and holding excludes another cast without requiring continued concentration.

Actual combat attack and defense, current Staff reach and skill, and physical damage remain canonical. The independent spell packet uses selected damage dice, ignores armor and hit location, and records its separate actual HP consequence. Physical strong/mastery and critical blow modifiers apply to the physical packet. Treating the triggered spell as a separate packet is a composed interpretation of B240's ordinary blow plus spell effect and B245's fixed spell expression; the printing does not explicitly give a Deathtouch-specific critical exemption.

The first victim subset is ordinary human with no relevant injury transformations. A locationless x1 HP adapter uses the normal injury pipeline; this is not a new printed damage-type classification or general Injury Tolerance exemption. Undead eligibility is printed but its specialized injury variants remain outside this first consumer. Unknown victims are rejected before attack dice and revalidated before physical settlement dice, rather than inventing a target at cast time. An initially living victim killed by that same accepted physical blow does not invalidate the already accepted magical contact.

A durable private association carries exact accepted charge identity across the real attack, defense and damage transactions. Magical settlement checks that accepted charge and event identity; physical consequences within the same transaction do not retroactively negate contact validated before the physical packet. Later or deferred commands revalidate the current carrier and target before their settlement dice. Dodge and weapon parry retain charge; an armor-ignoring spell can trigger through a successful block or unarmed parry. Parrying with the charged Staff retains it. Actual loss of wielding dissipates it. Joint-grip wrench delivery, ambiguous multiple-defense ordering, other Staff forms without current canonical weapon bindings, and wider source mechanics remain explicitly bounded until actual consumers prove them.

Public schemas remain closed. Original-genesis seeded replay, both-store current authority and retry/rollback proof, and actual manufacture-to-HP combat tests are required before acceptance; helper state and catalog flags do not establish the whole behavior.

Held continuity uses actual equipped, carried hand contact, independent of weapon readiness. Productive cast and attack/contact admission separately requires readiness and the selected hand; switching away from that hand retains the charge but this first delivery carrier refuses the switched-hand action explicitly. Canonical checkpoint diagnostics distinguish an unready/ready transition from a drop/retrieve transition; they are not fabricated inventory command receipts. Physical hand projection reconciliation must preserve an actually held unready weapon for the corresponding combat-host guarantee.

The private spell result retains its own HP before/after, injury, damage dice, and canonical injury-check traces with reasons. This keeps spell arithmetic inspectable without changing the physical InjuryTrace or exposing mana and construction proof internals.
