# Power project completion and Haste use

This bounded #785 proof starts with a lawful existing Haste clothing item and no Power enchantment. Both enchanters have approved builds containing the actual Enchant and Power prerequisite chains. Synthetic members of ten other colleges isolate Enchant's distinct-college prerequisite; they do not certify those spells. The found Haste binding and its magnitude are scenario facts. No Power binding, completed project, private effect, or execution receipt is supplied at genesis.

Campaigns fourth printing B480 prices Power levels 1 and 2 at 500 and 1,000 enchanting energy. B481 makes Slow and Sure contribute one energy per mage's eight-hour working day. Two mages therefore require 250 or 500 working days, finishing at 249 or 499 calendar days plus eight hours. The real `EnchantmentService` creates, begins, advances and settles each project. The lower approved Enchant/Power skill is 17; successful completion attaches a new provenance-bearing Power binding with that item Power and the purchased energy reduction. Slow and Sure spends no FP or HP at settlement.

The completed binding then drives the existing Haste consumer. In normal mana, Power 2 reduces one-level Haste's two-point casting cost to zero: the worn item gives Move 6 and Dodge 9 instead of 5 and 8, without a caster roll, FP payment or casting interval. Its effect survives a later 121-second wait and ends when its owner switches it off. Power 1 leaves a one-FP casting cost. A real approved item channel starts, concentrates and completes the two-second Haste cast; actual FP falls from 10 to 9, Move/Dodge become 6/9 and remain so past the first duration boundary without an upkeep payment while the wearer stays awake. Cancellation ends that effect. These expectations derive from B480/B482 and Characters third printing B251.

An independent natural-16 settlement fails against skill 17. B481's existing-enchantment exception preserves the Haste item, wastes the full working interval, adds no Power binding and grants no free wearer effect. The failed project leaves HP/FP unchanged. Success tests also reject an unauthorized settlement and a stale revision, and inject a failure after candidate checkpoint creation to prove that binding, project, receipts and event stream roll back together. Restarted exact retries do not create another binding or repeat costs. Every successful and failed project, subsequent use and ending command is folded and reexecuted from persisted seeds in SQLite and PostgreSQL. Registered campaign and stream projections omit the Power binding and reduction for the unrelated player and spectator, and omit private enchanting records for all players. This original test covers ordinary completed bindings.
