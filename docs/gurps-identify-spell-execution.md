# Bounded Identify Spell execution

Identify Spell has an actual approved-build Information consumer for the Basic Set B249 rule. Its existing IQ/Hard construction requires Detect Magic. This first subset admits purchased skill 10–14, authenticated normal mana and a trusted current physical touching observation for a co-located actor subject. It performs one explicit second of casting, resolves a secret check, and charges the full 2 FP on every rolled outcome, including critical success and failure. The initial subset permits one attempt per caster per game day; this is not a claim about every future Information or ceremonial carrier.

Spell truth comes from actual accepted producer commands and canonical spell events. Current casting uses the latest casting phase and its accepted start. Completed casting uses the initial successful completion event's actual clock timestamp, with an inclusive five-second window measured when Identify Spell completes. Ready deadlines, maintenance, release, cleanup and remembered results do not renew that window. All admitted on/by candidates are included. Fireball's future attack target is not treated as a subject with the spell on it: its caster is the supported observation route. Instantaneous effects can qualify even when their canonical phase is already ended. Permanent enchantments and automatic item wearer activations without an actual casting command are not new spell casts; actual paid item-produced casts on actors are distinct and require producer admission.

The server captures only relevant immutable accepted-producer evidence in the private recorded operation, outside the strict public command model. Seeded replay uses that captured evidence and rechecks its exact canonical event and actor/cast/spell identity. Normal execution derives the same evidence from the command's expected-revision checkpoint and accepted history, so later casts cannot change an exact retry's payload. Unsupported relevant casting producers and recent resisted casts refuse before dice rather than produce a partial answer. This includes current private Knowledge-college casts, successful recent Analyze Magic, Detect Magic or Identify Spell casts, participating active Enchant work and recent completed Enchant work. Canceled or failed historical private spell attempts do not become successful recent casts.

Wizards normally have heard of the listed Basic Set spells even when they have not purchased them. A trusted GM can identify a spell as unfamiliar and supply a general description. That physical/knowledge observation cannot declare which spells actually occurred. Success reports derive all admitted descriptions from the secret result, ordinary failure reports no finding, and critical failure requires an explicitly authored false report. True and false findings have the same audience shape. Canonical truth and dice remain private; unrelated players and spectators receive no findings. Findings are momentary recorded knowledge, not maintained effects.

The supported clock subset is healthy, noncombat, synchronous and free of timed hazard carriers and existing caster spells-on. Changed location, authority, actor availability or party activity refuses atomically. Temporal injury continuation, distant observation, concealment, ceremonial casting, skill-driven cost/time reductions, additional producer families and resisted completion semantics remain visibly unsupported. This consumer does not close all of #791: Detect Magic, Aura and broader Analyze Magic variants retain their own acceptance boundaries.

Focused tests use original approved genesis and actual Haste casting commands, secret success/ordinary failure/critical failure, unfamiliar descriptions, actual registered commands, fixed retries, restart, authority and late-CAS rollback. Producer tests independently measure completion age, maintenance, all on/by selection, ongoing Lockmaster, instantaneous Create Water and held Fireball after a lawful encounter ending. Actual private Information and Enchant producers exercise fail-closed completeness guards. SQLite and PostgreSQL replay verifies the original genesis, every accepted command and resulting canonical state; no spell effects or secret execution receipts are seeded.
