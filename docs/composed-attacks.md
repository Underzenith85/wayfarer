# Approved composed Innate Attacks (#764)

`apply_composed_attack` joins the approved Innate Attack purchase to the existing registered modifier projection, GURPS checks, and canonical injury/fatigue/Cyclic/Symptoms reducers. The player command selects an authored channel; its trusted `AttackCompositionContext` supplies the already committed maneuver, aiming time, geometry, target perception and defense choice. The command cannot invent another channel to reset cumulative source identity. Context is an internal simulation binding, not a player-authored modifier payload.

Characters third printing B61 defines one damage die per purchased level, Acc 3, 1/2D 10 and Max 100. This route rolls the purchased dice, uses the compiled specialty or B201 DX-4/cross-specialty defaults, applies the existing B550 range table and aiming bonuses, resolves ordinary Dodge, halves basic damage at or beyond 1/2D, and rejects range beyond Max. Armor Divisor changes actual DR penetration. Increased Range now changes both 1/2D and Max, correcting the existing projection to B106; Reduced Range retains its coupled and 1/2D-only modes. Accuracy changes actual aimed attack targets.

Malediction 1 uses the same registered adapter and resisted Quick Contest engine as creature special attacks, requires Concentrate and a perceived target, applies -1 per yard, bypasses conventional active defense and DR, and uses approved Will with the rule of 16. The Vision-Based limitation uses the B109 one-sense -20% cost, enforces available mutual vision, and consumes Protected Vision's B78 +5 resistance bonus. Creature construction and resolution are preserved.

Roll evidence retains actual attack, defense, resistance and damage dice. Revision and authority checks precede random resolution. Exact command retries return their prior consequence without rerolling or duplicating damage. Existing authored `apply_trait_attack` channels remain supported; composed damage is derived from the approved purchase rather than authored damage values.

Supported purchased routing currently includes Armor Divisor, Accurate/Inaccurate, Increased/Reduced Range, Malediction 1 with optional Vision-Based limitation, and the previously implemented Cyclic/Resistible and typed Symptoms paths. Other modifiers and unsupported variants fail visibly during approved construction. Fractional purchased damage dice, selective 1/2D-only or Max-only Increased Range, other Malediction tiers/senses, other attacks, powered loadout suppression, full combat posture/equipment/defense context, and critical-table consequences require their existing dedicated adapters or bounded follow-up work. This engine route does not certify all 87 modifier rows or establish end-to-end gameplay compliance; the conformance partitions and broader gates retain that distinction.

For active acute blindness from Symptoms (B124/B394), a declared Dodge is not evidence of awareness. The trusted composition context must separately describe the defender's current awareness of the incoming attack. Without that fact, a blind Dodge is rejected before random draws; with it, projected Dodge takes -4 exactly once. A context choosing no defense rolls no active defense. Ordinary composed attacks by a blind attacker still require a separate supported delivery consumer and are rejected. The lower authored-channel adapter consumes already adjudicated attack scores and rolls; its caller remains responsible for source-correct delivery, perception and sensory modifiers, especially for passive/contact attacks. Neither adapter is a public client authority.
