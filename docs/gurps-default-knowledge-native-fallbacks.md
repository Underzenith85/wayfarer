# Native physical and hazard default joins

Characters B173 permits an untrained default only where the skill is known in
the actor's society. Fresh generation-2 physical and hazard producers require
genuine current GM knowledge facts even before explicit actor adoption. Historical
generation 1 activates this restriction only after adoption; generation 0 retains
its earlier behavior. These hosts use the same private facts as other consumers.
A known fact permits an existing legal compiled default. It cannot manufacture
a missing catalog edge, remove acquisition prerequisites, pay Unusual Background,
or admit a forbidden definition. Purchased skills, including default-credit
improvements, retain their approved construction.

Required new Climbing, Hiking and Swimming attempts check this eligibility before
route resolution and initial health/exertion dice. Group hiking admits every new
daily contributor before any member's dice. Optional untrained Leadership cannot
supply the group bonus if unavailable. Existing daily Hiking outcomes and accepted
hazard schedules retain their captured numeric facts.

Unavailable optional skill contributions do not erase mandatory consequences.
Campaigns B355's swimming fatigue check uses the higher of HT or lawful Swimming;
when the latter is unavailable, the independent HT check still proceeds. B431
permits an Acrobatics attempt to mitigate a fall: without a lawful attempt, actual
falling injury still resolves with no invented failed skill roll. Jump distance
retains its independent native Basic Move basis if optional Jumping is unavailable.

New voluntary lifesaving and Diagnosis checks use a legal compiled skill before
care dice or FP. Their trusted care resolver is necessary to identify the bound
procedure; it supplies no numeric skill. A fresh direct drowning exposure likewise
requires lawful Swimming before creating a schedule. Unknown societal eligibility
is explicitly unsupported at this fresh source boundary; no numeric zero, automatic
failure or substitute drowning skill is invented. Already accepted schedules and
mandatory continuations remain unchanged.

Historical command generations 0 and 1 preserve their earlier native fallbacks,
seeded reexecution and exact retries. These joins do not certify separate B345
minimum-effective-skill behavior in the existing care or group-hiking consumers,
and do not cover new involuntary exposure classes or all societal biographies.
