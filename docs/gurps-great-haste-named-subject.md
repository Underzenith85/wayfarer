# Named, previously known Great Haste subjects

Basic Set B239 permits a Regular spell to name a subject who is not seen. The GM determines the actual range at the casting roll; failure to touch or see the subject adds −5, and physical barriers do not block a Regular spell. B251 still supplies Great Haste's three Concentrate maneuvers, cost, effect duration and end fatigue.

The private `named-great-haste` carrier implements a bounded named-actor route during an active canonical encounter. The caller selects an explicit canonical fact already known by the caster: its subject must be the channel's approved actor, predicate `name`, and value exactly the actor's canonical name. Generic promises, beliefs, commitment visibility, guessed identity, a supplied distance or a visibility boolean do not establish this binding. The accepted actor, name, fact contents, channel and caster remain immutable during the cast. Missing, changed or no longer known facts reject continuation before dice or payment. Canonical world authoring and existing actor control authorize these facts and commands; the casting service cannot create or grant knowledge.

Current subject pose, caster sight and board line of sight determine actual range and the unseen modifier at each binding, including the final roll. The canonical spell targeting ledger refreshes those modifiers at completion. A wall therefore adds the unseen penalty rather than denying this private route. Ordinary casting physiology, native ATR, concentration interruption, paid energy, effect expiry and canonical maneuver settlement remain in the existing host. Cancellation can stop an accepted cast after its name knowledge is lost.

Generation 3 is paired strictly with the new private typed carrier and authenticated original-input metadata. Generations 1 and 2, recorded metadata absence, public spell/combat schemas and the old private command adapter retain their previous admission. Older casting carriers cannot continue a named cast. A private origin witness is excluded from public projections and rejected in authored genesis. Exact retries retain the original bytes; seeded history folds and reexecutes using the private replay adapter.

This slice does not select an unknown or absent actor, guess a hidden location, resolve a description such as “the nearest person,” cast across encounter boundaries, combine named targeting with a selected Step, or activate Great Haste on the caster mid-turn. These carrier extensions remain unverified under [the remaining Movement host carriers (#980)](https://github.com/Underzenith85/wayfarer/issues/980). See `gurps-haste-construction-acceptance.md` for the written #797 baseline case map.
